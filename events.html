AirGap CTF.

Once a year we stop playing other people's challenges and write our own.

Twenty-four hours, jeopardy style, teams of up to four. Every challenge is written, tested and broken by members first, and every author is credited on the scoreboard. Open to every college in Delhi NCR, with an on-campus room for anyone who wants to play together.
