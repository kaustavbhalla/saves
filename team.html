Six domains.

Pick one to start. Most members end up in three.

  • Web exploitation

    How web apps trust the wrong input: injection, auth bugs, SSRF and the browser's own rules.

  • Reverse engineering

    Reading programs you have no source for, from crackmes to firmware.

  • Forensics

    What disks, memory and packet captures remember after the attacker leaves.

  • Cryptography

    Why the maths holds, and the implementation mistakes that make it fail anyway.

  • Networks and cloud

    Routing, misconfigured buckets and the defensive side: logs, alerts and hardening.

  • Design and outreach

    The posters, this website, the newsletter and the awareness drives. Security needs explaining.

How to join.

Recruitment for 2026–27 is open until 31 October. It takes about a week.

  1. Fill in the form

    Your name, branch and year, and one sentence on what you want to break.

  2. Solve one flag

    Any day's challenge on the home page counts. Stuck is fine; tell us how far you got.

  3. A short chat

    Fifteen minutes with two people from the core team. No trick questions.

  4. Your first Flag Night

    You sit with a mentor and solve something real by 7:30 pm.
