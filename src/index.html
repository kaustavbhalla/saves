Since a room of people broke Enigma , security has been a team sport. We are that room at USICT .

A room for breaking things.

Who we are

Students from every branch and year at USICT: B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA, and anyone who has wondered what really happens after they press Enter. You need curiosity and a laptop. Experience is optional.

What we do

Weekly CTF practice. Workshops on the web, binaries, forensics and networks. Talks from people who do this for a living. And once a year, a public CTF that we write ourselves.

Every week

4 regulars

  • Flag Night

    Fri, 5:30 pm

    Two hours of capture the flag in the lab. Newcomers pair with someone who has solved the challenge before.

    Bring a laptop with a Linux VM.

  • Reading Room

    Tue, 6 pm

    One write-up or paper, read together and argued over. This month: classic web bugs and how they were found.

  • Lab hours

    Wed, 4 pm

    Help with the setup nobody explains: Kali, Burp Suite, Ghidra, Wireshark and a sane home lab.

  • Help desk

    Every day

    Ask anything in the club Discord. Someone from the core team answers within the day.

Every semester

4 big ones

  • Workshops

    Four a term

    Three hours, hands on, one subject: web exploitation, reverse engineering, forensics or network defence.

  • Talks

    Monthly

    Alumni and practitioners on real incidents, research and careers, followed by questions they actually answer.

  • AirGap CTF

    January

    Our own 24-hour jeopardy CTF, written by members and open to every college in Delhi NCR.

    Challenge authors get credited on the scoreboard.

  • Awareness drives

    October

    Phishing drills and password clinics for the whole campus during Cybersecurity Awareness Month.
