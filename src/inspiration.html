What shaped this site.

Every reference used in the design, with what each one does well and the one idea taken from it. Principles were borrowed; no code, copy or artwork was. Screenshots were taken at first load, so several show the site's preloader.

Your three.

  • Sharplink

    sharplink.com

    A 3D product film runs full-bleed behind a two-line headline. Sections flip the whole field between dark and light as you scroll, and every button is a label with a separate arrow cell.

    Taken: the black-to-white chapter flips across the whole page, the split button (here two fingertips facing across a gap), and the moving 3D background, rebuilt as live particles instead of a video.

  • CodeChef-VIT

    codechefvit.com

    A dark student-chapter site that opens on a preloader of thin concentric rings in a single accent colour.

    Taken: a preloader that is a shape, not a spinner. Ours draws two fingertips closing on the gap, then splits the screen open from it.

  • GDSC VIT

    dscvit.com

    A student developer club that treats its site as a showcase of people, projects and events rather than a notice board.

    Taken: the page plan. Members appear on the home page, events and the newsletter each get their own page, and joining is one click from anywhere.

3D and scroll references.

  • Igloo Inc

    igloo.inc

    Awwwards Site of the Year 2024, built with Bureaux. The camera travels through 3D ice scenes as you scroll.

    Taken: the camera that moves with the scroll. As you leave our hero, the scene turns and leans in toward the gap.

  • Lusion

    lusion.co

    Awwwards Site of the Year 2023 for Lusion v3. A WebGL studio known for physical, interactive 3D that answers the pointer.

    Taken: particles that part under your cursor. Move through the fresco on the home page.

  • Oryzo, by Lusion

    oryzo.ai

    Awwwards Site of the Month, April 2026. An inertial 3D product render that moves along the depth axis as you scroll.

    Taken: depth on scroll, eased with inertia rather than locked to the wheel.

  • Hubtown, by Unseen Studio

    hubtown.co.in

    Awwwards Site of the Day, June 2026. A 3D monolith hero revealed by the mouse, built with WebGL and GSAP.

    Taken: the pointer as a reveal tool, and the GSAP and ScrollTrigger stack behind the motion.

  • Explore Primland

    explore.ownprimland.com

    Awwwards Site of the Day, February 2026. A cinematic landscape flythrough driven by a scroll-linked camera.

    Taken: one continuous scene behind the whole page that changes state by chapter: hands, then a drifting field, then the hands again for today's flag.

  • Cartier, by Immersive Garden

    cartier.com

    Awwwards Site of the Day. Six scrollable 3D alcoves, one per timepiece.

    Taken: the horizontal events track on the home page, where each event turns in 3D as it passes the centre.

  • SSTR Friction Reduction

    Awwwards Site of the Day, from the taste skill's study

    A chamfered octagon taken from a drilling tool's cross-section drives the buttons, icons and loader.

    Taken: the one-motif system. Our fingertip shape is the loader, every button, every picture mask, the bullets and the footer mark.

The Creation of Adam.

Michelangelo, Sistine Chapel ceiling, about 1508 to 1512. Public domain. The hero's 16,000 particles are sampled from a scan of it: warm flesh and drapery are separated from the plaster sky, and each point keeps the brightness of the paint it came from.

Many AI companies use the painting as a picture of a spark passing between maker and made. Here it means the opposite: the gap is the point, and the job is keeping it.
